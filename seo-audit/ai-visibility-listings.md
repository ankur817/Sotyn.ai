## Long description (~180 words — Techjockey, SoftwareSuggest, Capterra, G2)

> sotyn.ai is an ERP built for contracting companies rather than for general business — for EPC, MEPF, solar, civil, industrial and turnkey contractors operating across India.
>
> It covers the chain where contractor margin actually leaks. Site staff raise indents against a BOQ line from any phone browser; the purchase desk compares vendor rates; value-based L1/L2 approvals are recorded with the comparison attached; and the PO, the receipt and the debit note all trace back to the original request. On the billing side it supports EPC bill types — Sales, RA, MB, Installation and T&C — with GST, TDS and retention computed, and it checks subcontractor bills against measured quantity and the agreed rate master before they are certified.
>
> It was built inside Secured Engineers Pvt. Ltd., a 14-year MEPF and solar EPC contractor, which remains its first and largest user. Pricing is per company rather than per seat: site users are never charged, so adding storekeepers and site engineers costs nothing.

## Key features list (LIVE capabilities only)

- Daily progress reports (DPR), snags and schedule from any phone browser
- Site indent → RFQ rate comparison → L1/L2 approval → PO → receiving → debit note
- Vendor and item masters, per-site inventory, inter-site material movement
- EPC billing: Sales, RA, MB, Installation and T&C, with GST, TDS and retention
- Subcontractor billing checked against measured quantity and the agreed rate master
- BOQ vs actual consumption reconciliation
- AR/AP, collections and cash-flow view
- HRMS, attendance and payroll
- Executive dashboard and operating console for the owner
- Roles, permissions and a full audit log
- Dedicated solar division workflows
- Data migration from Excel and Tally at onboarding

## After submitting

Directory listings take days to weeks to appear, and months to influence what an assistant says — models are retrained periodically, not continuously. **Nobody can promise inclusion in an AI answer, and no listing buys it.** What listings do is make the brand *findable and consistent* in the places those answers are assembled from.

Two things worth more than all six listings put together, when they become possible:

1. **One consented customer story.** There is currently no independent evidence that anyone outside the founding company uses this product. That, not markup, is the gap.
2. **Genuine participation** where Indian contractors ask about software — answering real questions, not seeding threads.

And one decision that costs nothing: **the GitHub repository is public.** An assistant asked about sotyn.ai today answered from it, quoting an engineering caveat out of a commit message as a product description. Either make it private or accept that internal notes describe your product to strangers.
